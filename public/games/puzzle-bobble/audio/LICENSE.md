# Puzzle Bobble audio provenance

The audio files in this directory were selected from two asset packs by
[Kenney](https://kenney.nl). Both packs are released under
[Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
and may be used in personal and commercial projects without attribution.

Source: [Kenney UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio)
